About the Alliance

Consciousness Elevation Alliance (CEA) was formed to provide a reflective and ethical framework for understanding how modern systems influence human awareness and lived experience.

CEA examines the intersections of technology, creativity, education, and social systems through a long-term, human-centered lens. This website provides an informational public presence for the Alliance.

CEA serves as a conceptual and organizational foundation for future initiatives.

TMT

ThinkMyThought

ThinkMyThought (TMT) is a consciousness-centered AI company that helps people stay centered while offering a supportive space to develop and grow their ideas.

CCC

Cradles to Concious Creators

Cradles to Concious Creators (CCC) is a course for children and parents that teaches practices for healing, growth, and living from a place of wholeness and creativity.
